Common questions

I’ve been told I’ll get a LAAMP account. Where is it?

Your employer or site sets up your LAAMP account. When it’s ready, you’ll get a welcome email. It tells you how to create your account, download the app and set your password.

LAAMP can’t create an account for you. If the email hasn’t arrived, ask your site’s health, safety and training manager.

DraftSource: LAAMP Freshdesk article “1.2 Logging In & User Interface Overview”, last updated 4 Jul 2025.

What happens the first time I log in?

You’ll be asked to do three things:

  1. Set a new password. It needs at least 8 characters, with an uppercase letter, a lowercase letter and a number.
  2. Accept the disclaimer.
  3. Take a profile photo. You may also be asked to upload an ID, which is used only to verify who you are.

DraftSource: LAAMP Freshdesk article “1.2 Logging In & User Interface Overview”, last updated 4 Jul 2025.

I forgot my password. How do I reset it?
  1. Go to the LAAMP login page.
  2. Enter your username, then select Next.
  3. Select Forgot Password.
  4. Enter the email address linked to your account.
  5. Open the password reset email and log in with the temporary password in it.
  6. Set a new password when you’re asked to.

No reset email, or still locked out? Ask your site’s training coordinator.

DraftSource: LAAMP Freshdesk article “1.4 Profile Settings & Password Management”, last updated 1 Jul 2025.

How do I change my password?
  1. Log in to LAAMP and go to Settings.
  2. Select Change Password.
  3. Enter your current password, then your new one.
  4. Select OK to save.

DraftSource: LAAMP Freshdesk article “1.4 Profile Settings & Password Management”, last updated 1 Jul 2025.

Which version of the LAAMP app do I have?
  1. Log in to the LAAMP app.
  2. Open the side menu (the three lines at the top left).
  3. Look at the bottom of the menu. It shows your company’s name and the app version.

LAAMP will never ask for your password.

DraftSource: LAAMP Freshdesk article “How can I find what version of the LAAMP app I have?”, last updated 28 Nov 2022. This article is older, so check the steps still match the app.

Does LAAMP work without internet?

Yes. LAAMP works on any device, online or offline. Many tasks can be done with no connection, and your results sync when you’re back in range.

DraftSource: the FAQ on laamp.com.au/contact-us, not Freshdesk.

Where do I get the LAAMP app?

Download it from the App Store or Google Play. You can also use LAAMP in your browser.
